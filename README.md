## The pitch

> You have told me your app idea over coffee a dozen times — tonight, tell it once more in one plain sentence on make.fun and let an AI agent build your first version for free, so next time you can show me instead of telling me.

The whole entry is that single sentence: 226 characters, one line, aimed at
a friend who has been describing the same app idea for months but has never
written a line of code.

## Run it

```sh
bun main.ts
```

`main.ts` assembles the sentence from four named beats — the habit, the
ask, the build, and the payoff — checks that the result is still exactly
one sentence on one line, and prints it.

## Why this angle

Every app idea has a friend who has heard it a dozen times. The pitch does
not lecture that friend about technology; it just asks them to repeat the
story one more time, in writing, and promises the next telling will come
with something to show.
